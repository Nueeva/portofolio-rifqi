# Cinematic home

The home is an experience: one camera passes through the existing Tembus signal into the operator's real work. The official mark geometry remains owned by TembusMark.

The opening uses a sticky viewport with scroll-linked scale, rotation and a traveling light. The family origin changes the surface to paper, before dimensional plugin blocks lead into Apexsions and layered portfolio sheets introduce Portfolio 71. These are abstract geometry, not screenshots or product UI. The gateway scene resolves the narrative into contact.

Home palette: forest #101612, pale ink #edf0db, signal #d2ef68, origin paper #e8e5d6. Existing self-hosted Space Grotesk carries headings; Inter carries prose. Other public pages retain their established tokens. CSS is scoped under cinema and film classes.

No chapter labels, progress bars, repeated project card rails, invented metrics or replacement monograms. Content is present and opaque on first paint. Scroll motion is transform-only; reduced motion disables camera effects, sticky duration and ambient line animation. Public route transitions use a short visible camera lift instead of a hiding curtain.
